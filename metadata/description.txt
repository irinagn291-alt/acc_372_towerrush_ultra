Build a tower one moving platform at a time in TowerRush. Tap Place to align each platform with the one below. The overlap stays, the overhang is cut away, and a complete miss ends the climb.

Land perfect placements to build a streak. Every fifth consecutive perfect placement restores some platform width, up to its starting size. As your tower grows, the platforms move faster.

Choose a free climb or replay the daily climb, where reaching 20 floors completes the day's goal. Review your results and individual placements, including accuracy, offset and remaining width.

Unlock locations and finishes through your best climbs and perfect placements. Visit Profile to see cumulative statistics, personal bests and recent climbs, or revisit a result and its placement review.

Pause, resume or finish a climb early and save its current result. Adjust sound, haptics and reduced motion in Profile. VoiceOver actions let you place a platform or hear its alignment.

A small or medium widget shows a snapshot of your progress and opens the Play tab. TowerRush is available for iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The interface is in English.
