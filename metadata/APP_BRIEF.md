# Tower Rush

## What it is

Tower Rush is a single-player tower-stacking game. Players time each placement to build higher towers, improve their accuracy, and unlock locations and finishes.

## Launch and onboarding

A cold launch briefly shows “Preparing your skyline…”. On a first launch, the app opens directly to “Play”; there is no onboarding sequence.

The screen shows “BUILDER”, the starting location “Night City”, “A little higher.”, “Every time.” and an illustrated tower marked “YOUR NEXT SKYLINE”, “Start something tall” and “Illustration”. The initial “BEST FLOORS” and “BEST ACCURACY” are zero. Tap “Build” to begin a free climb or “Daily climb” to play the day’s shared sequence.

If saved progress cannot be opened, the app shows “Progress needs attention” and “Your saved progress could not be opened. Retry to keep your original file safe.” Tap “Retry loading” to try again.

## Screens

### “Play”

The tab shows the current location, the last tower after an attempt, personal best floors and accuracy, and the controls:

- “Settings”, the gear button, opens “Profile”.
- “Build” starts a free climb.
- “Daily climb” starts the daily sequence. Before completing the goal, it says “Reach 20 floors · one shared sequence”; afterward it says “Complete · build again anytime”.
- The bottom tabs “Play”, “Locations”, “Collection” and “Profile” switch between the main screens.

The screen also says “One tap to place. One more floor to chase.”

### Climb

A climb is labelled “FREE CLIMB” or “DAILY CLIMB” and shows the selected location, “FLOORS”, “PERFECT STREAK” and “WIDTH”. In the compact landscape layout, the streak label is “STREAK”.

- “Place” drops the moving platform. The overlapping part remains; overhang is cut away. A complete miss ends the climb.
- “Pause game” opens the pause screen. Leaving the app also pauses the climb.
- The moving platform offers the accessibility actions “Place platform” and “Hear alignment”. The latter announces “Aligned” or the distance and direction from alignment.

The first-placement instruction is “Tap when the edges line up”; afterward it is “5 perfect in a row restores a little width”. Feedback can include “Line it up. Make it count.”, “Today's shared sequence”, “Perfect placement”, “Perfect five · width restored”, a percentage followed by “aligned”, or “One more climb?”.

The pause screen says “Take a breath.” and “Your tower is right where you left it.” “Resume” continues the climb. “Finish attempt” opens “Finish this attempt? Your current result will be saved.” Choose “Save and finish” to record the result or “Keep building” to return to the paused climb.

### Result and “Placement review”

A finished free climb shows “CLIMB COMPLETE”; a daily result shows “DAILY CLIMB · [date] UTC”. It displays the tower and “FLOORS”, “ACCURACY” and “BEST STREAK”. The heading is “Find your rhythm.” for zero floors or “A new perspective.” otherwise. A completed daily goal says “Daily goal complete. Nicely built.”; other results say “Every placement is a little progress.”

- “Build again” starts another climb of the same type.
- “Review placements” opens “Placement review”.
- “Back to skyline” and the “Close result” button return to “Play”.
- A result opened from history has “Review placements” and “Close result”, but no “Build again” or “Back to skyline”.

“Placement review” says “Your climb, placement by placement.” and “Accuracy is the percentage of platform width that overlapped. Perfect placements count as 100%; a miss counts as 0%.” Each entry shows “Floor [number]” or “Missed placement”, its accuracy or “Perfect”, and alignment or remaining-width details. “Done” closes the review.

### “Locations”

The screen is headed “EXPLORE”, “Higher horizons” and “Four places to leave your mark.” It lists:

- “Night City” — “A thousand windows. One way up.”
- “Dune Valley” — “Warm horizons and quiet ambition.”
- “Frozen Cliffs” — “Clear skies. Sharper focus.”
- “Orbital Station” — “Your skyline, beyond the sky.”

An unlocked location offers “Build here”; tapping it selects that location for future climbs. The current location shows “Selected”. Locked locations show “Reach [number] floors to unlock” and progress toward the requirement.

### “Collection”

The screen is headed “COLLECTION”, “Make it yours” and “Earn every finish. Build your own style.” It shows a tower preview and four finish tiles: “Gilded”, “Jade”, “Glacier” and “Aurora”. Tap a tile to preview it, including when it is locked. When motion is enabled, the tower can also be dragged to turn it.

The preview instruction is “Drag to turn · tap a finish to preview”, or “Tap a finish to preview” with reduced motion. Tiles show “Unlocked” or “Achievement finish”. The selected finish’s requirement and progress appear below. “Use this finish” equips an unlocked finish; the currently equipped finish shows “Equipped”.

### “Profile”

The screen is headed “YOUR JOURNEY”, “Keep on climbing” and “Small improvements. Taller towers.” It shows “ATTEMPTS”, “TOTAL FLOORS” and “PERFECT”; under “Personal bests” it shows “Highest tower”, “Best accuracy”, “Perfect streak” and “Daily goals”.

Under “Make yourself comfortable”, the switches “Sound effects”, “Haptic feedback” and “Reduce motion” change those preferences. The screen explains: “System Reduce Motion is also respected. Platforms still move so you can time each placement.”

“Recent climbs” shows the “Last 50” completed attempts. Tap an attempt to open its result and placement review. “How to build” opens the rules; “Done” closes them. The rules cover “Line it up”, “Stay in the zone”, “Keep your focus”, “Take the daily climb” and “See your progress”.

The footer says “Made for offline play” and “Progress stays on this device. Daily climbs change at 00:00 UTC. No connection or account needed.”

### “Your skyline” widget

The small or medium widget shows “Your skyline”, personal-best floors and the next milestone. The medium widget also shows the number of climbs and best streak, or “Continue your tower · [number] floors” when a climb is unfinished. Tapping the widget opens the app on “Play”; an unfinished climb opens paused.

## Features

- “Build” free climbs and replayable “Daily climb” sequences shared for the UTC date.
- “Place” moving platforms, maintain a “PERFECT STREAK” and restore width with five perfect placements in a row.
- Pause, resume or finish an attempt, and review individual placements and accuracy.
- Track “Personal bests”, “Daily goals” and “Recent climbs”.
- Unlock and select four locations and four finishes.
- Adjust “Sound effects”, “Haptic feedback” and “Reduce motion”.
- View progress in the “Your skyline” widget.
- Play offline.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- A complete miss ends the climb. Use “Build again” to start another attempt.
- “Place” cannot be used while paused. Tap “Resume” first.
- “Daily climb” remains playable after its goal is complete; “Complete · build again anytime” confirms this. The daily sequence changes at “00:00 UTC”.
- Locked locations show “Reach [number] floors to unlock”: reach 10 floors for “Dune Valley”, 20 for “Frozen Cliffs” and 35 for “Orbital Station” in one attempt.
- Locked finishes can be previewed but cannot be equipped. Unlock “Jade” by making 30 perfect placements in total, “Glacier” by reaching 10 floors in one attempt, and “Aurora” by making 15 perfect placements in a row. “Gilded” is available initially.
- Before the first completed attempt, “Recent climbs” says “Your story starts with one floor.” “Start building” returns to “Play”; finish an attempt to add it to the list. The list retains the latest 50 attempts.
- If an attempt is finished before placing a platform, “Placement review” says “No placements yet” and “This attempt ended before the first platform was placed.” Place a platform in a subsequent attempt to see entries.
- If saving fails, the app says “Progress could not be saved. Keep the app open and try again.” Tap “Retry saving”.

## Starter content and resume

A fresh install starts with “Night City” and “Gilded” selected, zero records and no completed climbs. The tower on “Play” is an “Illustration”, not a previous result.

An unfinished climb is saved and can be resumed. On reopening the app it appears paused; tap “Resume” to continue.

## Permissions

None.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent.

## Data and support

Progress and preferences stay on this device. The app is designed for offline play. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

## Scanning and health

None. The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes and does not show health, medical or product-health information.

## Platform

The interface uses English and left-to-right layout, with British English date formatting. Daily climbs use the UTC date and change at “00:00 UTC”.

The app supports iPhone and iPad and requires iOS 17.0 or later. On iPhone it supports portrait and both landscape orientations; on iPad it also supports upside-down portrait.
